## 2. 摘要逐句功能

1. “Dataset distillation (DD) compresses a large training set into a small synthetic set for efficient training, but most DD methods optimize only clean accuracy and leave robustness uncontrolled.”（28词；`object_scope`、`problem_gap`）〔p.1，`“leave robustness uncontrolled”`〕
2. “Recent robust DD methods improve robustness, yet they often suffer from a poor accuracy–robustness trade-off because they (i) treat all adversarially perturbed examples uniformly, despite robust risk being dominated by near-zero robust margins, and (ii) do not explicitly increase inter-class separation in the decision boundary where attacks concentrate.”（57词；`problem_gap`）〔p.1，`“near-zero robust margins”`〕
3. “We present Contrastive Curriculum for Robust Dataset Distillation (C2 R), a framework that couples an attack-aware curriculum with a contrastive robustness objective.”（24词；`core_idea`、`method`）〔p.1，`“couples an attack-aware curriculum”`〕
4. “From a robust-margin perspective, we derive a perturbation score that approximates each sample’s robust hinge, enabling a curriculum that prioritizes the smallest-margin adversaries that most directly drive robust error.”（31词；`theory`、`method`）〔p.1，`“prioritizes the smallest-margin adversaries”`〕
5. “In parallel, a class-balanced contrastive robustness loss enforces adversarial invariance while explicitly widening boundary separation across classes.”（18词；`method`、`core_idea`）〔p.1，`“widening boundary separation”`〕
6. “Experiments on CIFAR-10/100, Tiny-ImageNet, and multiple ImageNet-1K subsets under six attacks show that C2 R achieves the best robust accuracy, outperforming prior robust DD by 2.8% on average.”（31词；`experimental_setup`、`quantitative_result`）〔p.1，`“2.8% on average”`〕
7. “Code is available at: https://github.com/SLGSP/CCR.”（7词；`impact_claim`）〔p.1，`“Code is available”`〕

功能顺序为对象/缺口→两个机制缺口→框架→AAC→CRL→实验数字→代码。摘要没有 seed、误差表达或 limitation 句。〔p.1，interpretation，`“outperforming prior robust DD”`〕

## 3. 引言推进

动作链为 **context → problem → failure_of_prior_work → missing_insight → core_idea → method_preview → contribution_list**。DD 先被定位为降低大规模训练存储/训练成本的压缩工具；随后指出 distilled data 继承 adversarial vulnerabilities；既有 robust DD 被概括为 meta-learning、curvature regularization 和 information-bottleneck 路线。〔p.1 §1，`“inherit not only the predictive signal”`〕

引言的关键缺口是 margin misprioritization 与 boundary neglect。前者把所有 adversarial companions 均匀处理，后者只做 class-mean alignment，未在竞争类别附近显式扩大分离。作者因此提出问题：能否聚焦 smallest-margin adversaries、提高 boundary separation，同时保持成本可行。〔p.2 §1，`“two structural gaps”` / `“Can we design”`〕

四项贡献依次是 robust-margin view、AAC+LS-PGD、CRL+class-balanced queue、跨 CIFAR/Tiny-ImageNet/ImageNet subset 与多攻击评测。贡献列表具体可证伪，且未新增摘要外数字。〔p.2 §1，`“Our contributions are fourfold”`〕

## 4. 相关工作

§2 位于页 2–3。第一簇按 bilevel、gradient matching、trajectory matching、distribution matching 分类 DD；第二簇按 meta-learning、robust classifier、curvature、group DRO、ROME 分类 robust DD。〔p.2 §2，`“Early DD is formulated as bilevel optimization”`〕

## 5. 方法与理论

§3 定义 DD 的 bilevel objective，内层在 synthetic set 上训练，外层在原分布评估；robust DD 的既有项是 clean/adversarial class-mean embedding matching。〔p.3 §3，`“standard bilevel formulation”`〕

§4.1 定义 logit margin、威胁集、robust margin `m(x;θ)=minδ gθ(x+δ)`、robust risk 与 hinge `[1−m]+`。由于 hinge 对 margin 单调递减，最大 per-sample hinge 等于最小 robust margin，成为 AAC 的排序依据。〔pp.3–4 §4.1，`“arg max v_i is exactly”`〕

AAC 用 PGD/LS-PGD 产生 adversarial companions，缓存 warm start；若一次 forward 检查不足，则一次 backward 加几何候选的 forward-only search。score `s(x)` 是估计 robust hinge，样本按 score 降序进入 curriculum batches。〔p.4 §4.2，`“forward-only search”`〕

CRL 将 clean anchor、自身 adversary 与同类样本设为 positives，把其他类的近邻设为 hard negatives。class-balanced FIFO queue 和低维随机投影将负例检索从 `O(M²)` 降为 `O(Mk)`，full embedding 仍用于最终 contrastive loss。〔p.5 §4.3，`“reduces ... from O(M 2) to O(M k)”`〕

总目标为 `L_C2R=(1−η)L_perf+ηL_CRL`。Algorithm 1 在 Appendix A 完整展开 real mini-batch、LS-PGD、score 排序、synthetic adversary、queue 更新和 SGD 更新。〔p.6 §4.4；p.11 Appendix A，`“Form total loss”`〕

理论属于解释/诊断而非 formal guarantee。编号公式为 (1)–(17)，(1)–(5) 为 DD/robust DD 预备式，(6)–(9) 为 margin 理论，(10)–(17) 为 PGD、score、CRL 与联合目标。页 15 Appendix E 另有 tail-weighted hinge 的 2 个未编号显示式。〔pp.3–6、15，layout_observation，`“Eqn. 9 formalizes”`〕

## 6. 实验设计

数据为 CIFAR-10/100、Tiny-ImageNet、六个 ImageNet-1K 10-class subsets；Appendix C.3 另有 ImageNet-10。网络是 CIFAR 的 three-block ConvNet 与高分辨率 ConvNet-D4/D5，ImageNet images resize 为 128×128。〔p.5 §5.1；p.11 Appendix B.1，`“six subsets”`〕

主文比较 MTT、SRe2L、D4M、ROME；Appendix B 解释 GUARD/RDD，Appendix C.3 用 RDD 做 shift 对照。主文攻击为 FGSM、PGD、CW、VMIFGSM、Jitter、AutoAttack，除 AutoAttack 外主文预算为 `ℓ∞ ε=2/255`；Appendix B.2 另描述 Square、MIM，并扩展 ε=4/255、8/255。〔pp.5–6、12–14，`“six widely used attacks”`〕

训练使用 SGD、learning rate 0.01、momentum 0.9、weight decay 5×10^-4；正文称 IPC=1/10/50，但 Table 1 实际含 1/5/10/30/50。表格结果均为 five independent runs 的平均值，没有 seed、标准差、区间、检验或多重比较校正。〔p.6 Table 1；p.8 Table 3，`“averaged over five independent runs”`〕

指标包括 clean/test accuracy、攻击 accuracy、drop rate `DR=(Acc_original−Acc_robust)/Acc_original×100%`、all-attack average robust accuracy 与 distillation time。没有 train/test deduplication、synthetic-to-test overlap 审计、完整硬件/命令或逐 seed 结果。〔pp.6–8 §5；pp.11–12 Appendix B，interpretation，`“official split”`〕

## 8. 消融、负面结果与限制

Table 3（IPC=10、四数据集）关闭 AAC/CRL 时 robust accuracy 为 24.81/9.40/1.43/23.76；AAC-only 为 26.73/11.45/2.73/25.27；CRL-only 为 26.04/10.98/2.10/24.65；两者均开为 28.41/12.30/3.74/26.62。两组件均启用的协同结果最高。〔p.8 Table 3，`“both simultaneously”`〕

Figure 6 显示 η=0.4 对 IPC=1/10 最优，η=0.6 对 IPC=50 更优，最佳附近有 plateau。Table 4 比较 PGD 与 LS-PGD，四数据集平均 robust accuracy 分别为 28.63/12.54/3.92/26.82 与 28.41/12.30/3.74/26.62，LS-PGD speedup 为 1.4×。这是明确的轻微 accuracy cost。〔p.8 §5.3/Table 4，`“1.4×”`〕

限制包括 moderate-resolution image classification、standard architectures、未测试 dense prediction/更广 backbone/graph/continual learning；Appendix E 承认 smallest margin 是 optimization interpretation，不是直接 measurement claim，robust-margin statistics/tail distributions 留待未来。〔p.15 Appendix D–E，`“it remains unclear”` / `“rather than a measurement claim”`〕

不利信息呈现策略包括 appendix migration、平均值加逐 setting 表格、明确 budget scope、robustness-oriented baseline scope、representative qualitative case，以及主动披露 IPC 略降、LS-PGD accuracy cost 和直接 margin measurement 缺口。〔pp.7–9、11–15，`“clearly scope our claims”`〕

## 11. 用词与可迁移判断

高频实词为 `robust`、`margin`、`adversarial`、`accuracy`、`attack`、`dataset`、`distillation`、`boundary`、`synthetic`、`loss`、`curriculum`、`contrastive`。高频短语包括 `robust margin`、`robust risk`、`decision boundary`、`attack-aware curriculum`、`contrastive robustness loss`、`class-balanced memory queue`。强动词集中于 `derive`、`prioritize`、`enforce`、`widen`、`achieve`；弱化词为 `often`、`most`、`around`、`slight`、`suggesting`、`remains unclear`。〔pp.1–8、15，interpretation，`“smallest robust margins”`〕

单一主线是把 robust DD 的均匀 alignment 改成由 smallest-margin tail 驱动的 AAC，并用 CRL 处理 boundary-level class competition。最有效模式是 Figure 1 的问题图→Eq. (6)–(9) 的可排序 surrogate→Figure 2 的流程→Table 3 的组件消融。〔pp.1、3–5、8，interpretation，`“Eqn. 9 formalizes”`〕

可迁移规则：当失败风险由 worst-case tail 控制时，让 per-example surrogate、训练调度、机制损失和主评测共享同一 tail 语义。适用边界是必须存在明确 threat set、可排序 failure surrogate 和可执行的 outcome metric；本文不能推出高分辨率 transfer、用户部署收益、隐私保证或 direct margin measurement。〔p.15 Appendix D–E，`“future work”`〕
