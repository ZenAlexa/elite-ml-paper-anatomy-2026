## 跨对象系统判断

- **visual_narrative**：Figure 1 defines aspect selection and evaluation; Figure 2 establishes FRABench composition; Figures 3–5 and Tables 2–7 carry OOD, judge, multi-aspect and DPO claims; Figures 6–11 audit split balance/diversity; Figures 12–15 expand per-aspect and scale results; Figures 16–22 document human annotation reliability and aspect distinctness; Tables 58–75 expose prompts and qualitative successes/failures; Figure 36 closes the DPO image-generation story.
- **caption_system**：Captions consistently begin with Figure/Table label and title. Main result captions define split or metric but often state no denominator/uncertainty; appendix captions provide part numbers and pointer-like context. Figure 3 and 15 captions explain held-out encoding; Table 3 defines tau/diff; prompt and qualitative captions identify task/model provenance but not sampling.
- **table_header_system**：Numeric tables use multi-level headers for task, benchmark, split and metric; best/second-best values use bold/underline and missing values use dashes. Taxonomy tables use stable aspect/definition/source columns. Prompt cards and qualitative comparison tables depart from numeric headers but preserve task/modality or evaluator provenance in the first row/column.
- **method_result_ablation_link**：The aspect-tree/pairwise-label interface in Figure 1 and Tables 13–23 feeds the FRABench split in Figure 2/Table 12, which is tested by the generalization radar/Table 2 and per-aspect Tables 33–48; Figure 5 and Tables 50–52 test joint learning; Tables 6–7 and Figure 36 test DPO use.
- **main_appendix_link**：Main Figures 1–5 and Tables 1–7 state the resource, generalization, judge and downstream outcomes. Appendix Figures 6–22 and Tables 8–75 provide balance, aspect definitions, exact per-aspect values, prompts, reliability, correlations and selected qualitative cases; references to these objects are frequent but some captions require body text for denominators and exclusions.
- **typography_consistency**：Vector table/body typography is stable, but embedded raster figures have smaller sans-serif labels and dense appendix tables fall below comfortable reading size. UI/prompt screenshots are legible only with zoom, and taxonomy/heatmap abbreviations require cross-reference.
- **color_consistency**：Color is categorical within each figure family rather than globally method-stable. Held-out black/blue sectors and diverging heatmap colors have local semantics; table ranking is grayscale-safe through bold/underline, whereas bars/radar methods remain color-dependent.

## 最终判断

- **most_reusable_patterns**：
  - 将 aspect/task held-out 单元直接画入雷达 spoke 或黑/蓝 sector，并以 Table 2/29 的聚合行复核。
  - 用固定多层表头和 bold/underline 规则把 benchmark、task、metric 与 split 保持可扫描对齐。
  - 以 Figures 6–11 的组成/多样性诊断、Figures 16–18 的人类标注流程/IAA 和 Figures 19–22 的相关矩阵补足主结果的可审计边界。
  - 将 prompt/interface 与 qualitative case 作为可复现机制证据，与 DPO 数值结果分开。
- **highest_value_objects**：
  - Figure 1：把 UA/TA aspect selection、模态路由和 evaluator 输出压缩成方法接口。
  - Figure 3 + Table 2：同屏表达 task/aspect generalization 的 held-out 设计、聚合结果和黑色 unseen-TAs 语义。
  - Figure 4：暴露强、普遍差和弱 aspect，避免只报告平均准确率。
  - Tables 50–52、Figures 18–22：分别把联合学习异质性、人类可靠性和 aspect distinctness 的边界显式化。
- **failure_patterns**：
  - 多数 numeric/radar/bar 对象只给 point average，不给 seed、分母或运行/标注不确定性。
  - 雷达和多组柱状图在缩小或灰度下依赖颜色，密集 appendix 标签和热图缩写降低可读性。
  - 联合 task/aspect 配置同时改变数据范围与训练条件，视觉对象不能单独证明 synergy 的因果机制。
  - qualitative tables/Figure 36 是选择性案例，没有 prevalence 或 selection protocol，不能代替总体效果。
- **one_sentence_visual_strategy**：论文先用 aspect-tree/FRABench 流程图定义统一评测接口，再用 held-out radar、分组表和 aspect bars 证明迁移与比较，最后用附录的组成、可靠性、相关性、prompt 和 qualitative 对象限定这些 point-estimate 结论的适用边界。
