## 6. 最终判断

- **最可复用模式**：
  1. Theory-to-evidence small multiples: align parameter sweeps, share a response quantity, and distinguish continuous prediction, empirical points, and replication spread.
  1. Stable method-by-regime table matrices: keep rows and column semantics fixed as generator, architecture, feature extractor, or sample regime changes.
  1. Mechanism companion diagnostics: use a directly labeled selector bar chart or a metric matrix to explain why downstream accuracy changes.
  1. Use explicit grouped headers and task-specific captions for crossed robustness and ablation conditions.
- **最高价值对象**：Figure 1；Table 1；Table 10；Figure 2；Table 13
- **主要失败模式**：
  1. Appendix captions frequently omit denominators, seed counts, feature details, and operational definitions of “on par”.
  1. Dense tables and repeated legends rely on very small text and require surrounding prose to recover the design.
  1. No global color identity or explicit best/second-best marking links Covariance matching across tables and figures.
  1. Figure 2 uses unequal x-axis maxima and lacks uncertainty, weakening cross-panel quantitative comparison.
- **一句话视觉策略**：A compact vector-first system uses one theory small multiple, repeated accuracy matrices for controlled robustness, and a directly labeled leak/metric diagnostic to move from theoretical covariance alignment to empirical downstream and mechanistic evidence.
