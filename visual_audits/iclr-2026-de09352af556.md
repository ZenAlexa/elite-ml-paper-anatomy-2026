## 最终判断

- **最可复用模式**：Use a threshold plus shaded feasible band and redundant point symbols for a theoretical safety region.；Pair a pipeline overview with a magnified implementation mechanism and its surrogate equation.；Keep headline metrics, relative Δ, and operational Cost on one table decision surface.；Reuse a fixed seed-panel template across reward, rate, failure, and cost metrics, with a defined across-seed summary.；Pair evaluator ablation tables for absolute error and ranking accuracy, using identical rows and columns.。
- **最高价值对象**：Figure 2 for the evaluator/planner/synchronous-coupling interface.；Tables 1–3 for the simulated, real-world, and unseen-advertiser headline comparisons.；Table 4 plus Figure 3 for constraint attribution and qualitative failure evidence.；Figures 4–5 and Table 5 for Lipschitz and evaluator reliability checks.；Figure 8 plus Figure 9 and Tables 9–10 for reproducible evaluator design choices.。
- **失败模式**：Most tables provide point estimates without repeat counts or uncertainty, including the main A/B comparisons.；Captions often omit denominator, assignment, split, or aggregation details.；Figures 4–5 are title-only and do not state the 8,000-pair sampling in the caption.；Individual seed curves support a stability claim without a summary statistic.；Figure captions 10–12 contain the PDF spelling “AIGB-Peral”, and Figure 3’s filled emphasis is not defined as non-uncertainty.。
- **一句话视觉策略**：The paper uses compact pastel theory/method diagrams, black-and-white metric tables, paired constraint diagnostics, and multi-seed appendix curves to connect safe generative planning to performance gains, while leaving protocol and uncertainty metadata thinner than the visual claims require.
