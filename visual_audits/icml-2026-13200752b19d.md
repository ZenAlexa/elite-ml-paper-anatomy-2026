## 全文视觉风格
- **结构**：正文 Figures 1–4 建立攻击、数据集、机制和构建流程，Table 1/Figure 5 给出主结果，Figure 6/Table 2 给出 TJA/VJA paired comparison，Figure 7/Table 3 给出 reasoning/judge ablation；附录 Figures 8–19 与 Tables 4–8 负责 taxonomy、qualitative output/failure、sensitivity、judge distributions、cost/agreement/generalization、prior-benchmark comparison、format 和 model roster。
- **字体**：PDF captions/tables 主要为 Nimbus Roman/Computer Modern math，表格 body/header 约 7–9 pt；图内定量标签约 4–8 pt，网页/PNG 图中另有 Arial/Impact/monospace/手写风 raster 字样。精确 PDF font object 与 raster 内字体不混为一谈。
- **色彩与输出**：TJA/VJA 使用蓝/绿，风险层级使用有序 pastel，reasoning 使用蓝/绿，unsafe/default/positive influence 重用红色，format/benchmark composites 使用 cyan/neutral backgrounds。标签、直接数字、位置、箭头和行列提供部分冗余，但小图和红绿文字灰度可读性有限。
- **一致性与缺口**：双栏论文外壳、caption 位置和极简横线稳定；最常见缺口是 error-bar/± 的统计类型、分母/重复定义、模型 panel mapping、定性样本选择、runtime 单位和 source/version 绑定。

## 跨对象系统判断
- **Visual narrative**：Figures 1–4 move from the VJA/TJA threat interface to benchmark statistics, defense mechanism and curation pipeline. Table 1 and Figure 5 then establish the main category/risk-level comparison; Figure 6 and Table 2 pair a qualitative case with TJA/VJA deltas; Figure 7 and Table 3 isolate reasoning and judge effects. Appendix Table 4/Figure 8 fix the taxonomy and exemplars, Figures 9–12 provide qualitative outputs and failures, Figures 13–15 test visual-prompt sensitivity, Figures 16–17 expose judge-dependent HS distributions, Tables 5–7 cover complexity/agreement/generalization, Figure 18 positions the benchmark against prior work, and Figure 19 fixes the file format.
- **Caption system**：Captions consistently begin with a descriptive title and then identify the object’s setup or comparison. Figures 1, 6 and Table 1 make directional claims or rankings; most other captions are descriptive. Captions often omit denominator, model-panel mapping, exact aggregation and uncertainty definitions. Captions for Figures 9/10, Figure 17 and Table 1 contain naming/count ambiguities that are visible only when the rendered object is compared with the PDF text.
- **Table header system**：Tables use compact single-level headers, upward/downward metric arrows, model/category columns and minimal/booktabs-like horizontal rules. Table 1 adds model/metric hierarchy and cell color/ranking glyphs; Tables 2–3 use paired rows or judge rows; Appendix Tables 4–8 switch to taxonomy, cost, agreement, transfer and model metadata. Header direction is useful, but the switch from ↑ metrics in the main table to ↓ metrics in Table 7 needs a caption-level reminder.
- **Method/result/ablation**：The visual-only attack interface in Figure 1 and the benchmark vocabulary in Figures 2/4 are instantiated by Table 1/Figure 5. Figure 3’s safety trigger maps to Figure 7’s reasoning ablation, Table 5’s overhead and Table 7’s transfer. Figure 6/Table 2 quantify the TJA/VJA contrast introduced in Figure 1, while Table 3 and Figures 16–17 test whether judge choice changes the evidence. Figures 13–15 connect controlled prompt alterations to robustness outcomes.
- **Main/appendix link**：Main Table 1/Figure 5 summarize all categories and risk levels; Appendix Table 4/Figure 8 provide definitions and exemplars, Figures 9–10 provide output-level cases, and Figures 16–17 provide judge distributions. Main Figure 3 and Figure 7 are extended by Table 5, Table 7 and Table 6. Figure 4’s curation path is completed by Figure 19’s storage format and Table 8’s evaluated roster.
- **Typography**：PDF tables and captions use embedded Nimbus Roman/Computer Modern-style objects at approximately 7–9 pt; figure labels are commonly approximately 4.5–8 pt after embedding. Exact linked PNG assets carry their own raster typography and cannot establish a paper-wide font rule. The result is a stable article shell with mixed graphic lettering and occasional monospace/code styling.
- **Color**：Blue/green remains the main TJA/VJA and reasoning contrast; risk-level bars use ordered pastel colors; red is reused for unsafe/default/positive emphasis and blue/green for safe/negative or open-model cues. Labels, direct values, row/column position and arrows add redundancy, but red/green sensitivity text, tiny pastel qualitative panels and overlapping commercial/open palettes weaken grayscale and color-vision robustness.

## 最终判断
- **最可复用模式：
  - Figure 1’s conceptual attack comparison plus direct-delta grouped bars gives a compact threat-to-headline bridge; a paired table then carries the full metric detail.
  - Figure 2/4/8/19 form a reusable benchmark chain: scale cards and examples, curation provenance, one exemplar per taxonomy class, and machine-readable storage format.
  - A fixed model/category matrix with direct HS/Denied labels (Figures 9–10) complements aggregate metric tables without pretending qualitative cases are prevalence estimates.
  - Figures 13–15 connect one-factor visual perturbation examples, default-relative influence summaries and box/dot distributions to make robustness claims interpretable.
  - Figures 16–17 reuse identical discrete-score small multiples under two judges, allowing judge dependence to be visually compared and then quantified in Tables 3/6.
- **最高价值对象：
  - Table 1: the primary category-by-metric safety decision surface across 1054 IESBench images and eight model configurations.
  - Figure 1: the clearest high-level explanation of why VJA is different from TJA and where the headline ASR contrast comes from.
  - Figure 4: the strongest dataset-reproducibility object because it combines risk taxonomy with collection, quality control, prompting and evaluation steps.
  - Figures 16–17: matched judge-distribution panels that expose the dependence of HS evidence on evaluator choice.
  - Figure 19 plus Table 8: compact reproduction anchors for data layout and evaluated model roster.
- **失败模式：
  - Captions frequently show exact-looking values without defining denominator, replicate aggregation or uncertainty; Figure 7 error caps and Table 3 HS± are especially underspecified.
  - Dense composites, qualitative grids and small multiples rely on tiny labels, pastel/red-green color and long model names; reduction and grayscale materially weaken inspection.
  - Figure 9’s caption/page row-range continuity, Figure 17’s eight-versus-nine model count and Table 1’s model-name wording create avoidable audit ambiguity.
  - Table 5 omits runtime units and Figure 14 omits the formal definition of maximum/extreme influence, limiting direct reproducibility of efficiency/robustness claims.
  - Qualitative failure and benchmark-comparison objects are useful mechanisms/examples but provide no sample-selection rule or prevalence, so they cannot independently support aggregate safety conclusions.
- **一句话视觉策略：**The paper uses a recurring VJA/TJA, I1–I15, risk-level and judge vocabulary to move from threat interface and dataset construction through category metrics, paired qualitative cases, defense/robustness ablations, judge distributions and file-format/model-roster details, with strong structural reuse but incomplete uncertainty and provenance definitions.
