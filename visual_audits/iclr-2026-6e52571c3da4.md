- **Caption:** “Figure 1: RefineStat workflow: (1) A user provides data and prompt to the language model, which generates a probabilistic program. (2) Constrained semantic decoding enforces syntactic and semantic validity of the generated program. (3) A Bayesian reliability check diagnoses convergence, divergences, and predictive validity. If failures are detected, the model is refined by backtracking and re-sampling priors or likelihoods. (4) Upon passing checks, we get final reliable probabilistic program.” (69 whitespace-delimited words.) It moves `title`, `setup`, `encoding_key` and `comparison`; the title is not bold; it is not fully self-contained and does not state a quantitative finding.
- **Typography:** embedded raster labels mix serif-like headings, sans-serif-like labels and code-like text, with regular/bold and occasional italic/math forms. Rendered sizes are approximately 5–15 pt (`rendered_estimate`, medium confidence); caption/body font is Nimbus Roman No9 L (`pdf_object`).
- **Color:** mixed, approximately `#D1EDF9`/`#34749C` for constrained generation, `#F7E4D8`/`#DA7842` for the reliability check, `#FFFFAC`/`#FFFF54` for prior/likelihood labels, `#EA5E5B` for rejection, `#54A231` for acceptance, and gray/black/white neutrals. Shape, labels and arrows provide partial redundancy, but status remains color-dependent; it is not grayscale-safe.
- **Complexity:** score 4; one panel, many process/code subcomponents, roughly 14 annotations and 20 visible marks. There is no separate legend box; labels are direct.
- **Plot grammar:** raster; x/y scales `none`; no grid, legend, hatching, reference line or uncertainty; direct labels; about two marker families (cross/check icons) and two line/arrow treatments; approximate stroke 1.2 pt (`rendered_estimate`).
- **Encodings:** x is workflow progress from data/prompt through generation and reliability to output; y separates inputs, fragments, refinement and acceptance; color marks semantic/diagnostic roles; rounded boxes, dotted code boxes, arrows and cross/check shapes mark states; solid arrows encode flow; text names predicates and diagnostics.
- **Data/statistics:** conceptual method interface, not a quantitative sample. It shows six semantic predicates, prior/likelihood fragments and Bayesian checks for R-hat, ESS, sampler health, convergence, divergences and predictive validity. No observations, denominator, estimate or uncertainty interval is encoded.
- **Evidence relation:** Section 3, Definitions 2–4, the six predicates in Section 3.1 and Algorithm 1 formalize the pipeline depicted here. The figure supplies structural method evidence for the run-rate, diagnostic and ablation results in Tables 1–6.

- **Method/result/ablation link:** diagrams map predicates and reliability gates to Tables 1–6; Table 4 isolates predicates; Table 7 gives code-level semantic cases; Table 8 bounds cost; Table 9 stress-tests memorization.
- **Main/appendix link:** main objects cover method, PyMC outcomes, external comparison, ablation and NumPyro; appendix tables deepen semantic filtering, token efficiency and prompt robustness. No appendix figure is physically present.
- **Typography:** Nimbus Roman/Computer Modern establish the document baseline, but dense tables are about 6.67 pt and diagrams are heterogeneous raster compositions.
- **Color:** local red/green or pastel diagram palettes do not carry into monochrome tables or persist as a cross-figure method key; the status classes are not fully grayscale-safe.

## Final judgment

### Most reusable patterns

1. Labeled method-interface schematic joining prompt/data, semantic gating, diagnostic gating, refinement and acceptance.
2. Stable grouped Mean/Std diagnostic headers with dataset blocks and explicit failure glyphs.
3. Complete-system plus leave-one-component-out ablation rows with a delta column.
4. Per-dataset cost means/dispersion plus normalized token ratio and matched memorization-stress variants.

### Highest-value objects

- **Figure 1:** strongest method-interface explanation.
- **Table 2:** highest-value diagnostic/reliability comparison.
- **Table 4:** clearest mechanism attribution.
- **Tables 8–9:** strongest cost and memorization boundary evidence.

### Failure patterns

- The automatic source inventory’s “exact” candidate is unrelated; the linked repository has no validated paper visual/LaTeX source.
- Dense 6.67 pt diagnostic tables omit run/threshold/denominator contracts and are hard to audit.
- Raster figures rely on local colors and heterogeneous typography with weak grayscale behavior.
- Captions are descriptive rather than self-contained; Table 2’s metric count conflicts with nearby prose.
- Repeated Mean/Std text without deltas or graphical uncertainty makes large variant tables laborious.

### One-sentence visual strategy

Use two compact colored raster schematics to explain semantic gating and diagnostic refinement, then monochrome grouped Mean/Std tables for run-rate, quality, ablation and backend claims, with appendix tables extending the same grammar to semantic examples, token cost and memorization stress tests.
