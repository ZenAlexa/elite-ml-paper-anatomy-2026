# Visual audit: iclr-2026-3b8d372687ad

## Scope and audit basis

Paper: Veritas: Generalizable Deepfake Detection via Pattern-Aware Reasoning. The official proceedings PDF is the Figure/Table inventory authority. All 58 pages were rendered at 200 dpi (1700 x 2200 pixels) and inspected, including body, references, appendix, qualitative floats, and prompt-template pages. The PDF inventory contains 40 figures and 19 tables; it has no numbered algorithms.

The source check followed the reading record, source inventory, local visual-source inventory, and GitHub. The selected repository is https://github.com/EricTan7/Veritas, checked on main at commit d384e81dc73f838a3a0c8c1a44d9b8976bd5788b. Three local acquired plotting scripts are listed in the JSON. They provide related plot conventions but no paper-specific generator for every object, so object-level styling and content are grounded in the 200 dpi PDF render.

## Object inventory

Main figures: Figures 1–6. Main tables: Tables 1–8. Appendix figures: Figures 7–40. Appendix tables: Tables 9–19. The PDF controls this count; the automated rows that share a page or caption region were split into their individually labeled objects.

## Algorithms and non-figure blocks

The PDF contains no numbered algorithms. Equations and prompt cards are treated as surrounding evidence rather than added to the figure/table arrays. Prompt cards in Figures 28–40 are audited as screenshot/protocol objects because the schema has no prompt-specific type.

## Final judgment

- Most reusable patterns:
  - Dataset overview combining source collection, evaluation protocol, split composition, and baseline behavior.
  - Two-stage training schematic with data, model, human/reward, and output objects aligned along a causal flow.
  - Cumulative line/grouped-bar ablations with direct value labels and a fixed split order.
  - Qualitative input-plus-tagged-reasoning cards paired with quantitative accuracy or reasoning-quality tables.
  - Boxed prompt artifacts that preserve exact output tags for reproducibility.
- Highest-value objects:
  - Figure 2: the dataset/protocol overview gives the core split logic and baseline gap in one view.
  - Figure 3: the two-stage training pipeline binds SFT, MiPO, P-GRPO, and reward flow.
  - Figure 9: the annotation taxonomy, staged generation, and examples make pattern-aware supervision concrete.
  - Table 1: the wide hierarchical benchmark table is the primary quantitative generalization evidence.
  - Figure 1 and Figure 6: qualitative reasoning cards make the transparency claim inspectable.
- Failure patterns:
  - Long qualitative reasoning and prompt cards are below comfortable page-scale readability.
  - Captions frequently omit sample selection, prompt/version details, fixed workload parameters, and uncertainty or run-count definitions.
  - Very wide benchmark tables require tiny typography and numerous abbreviations, limiting auditability.
  - Pastel colors provide local grouping but are not globally stable or reliably grayscale-safe.
  - Qualitative examples can illustrate faithfulness or failure but lack denominators and selection protocols.
- One-sentence visual strategy: Veritas uses a progression from qualitative detector-output contrast to dataset/protocol and two-stage reasoning schematics, quantitative split-wise ablations, and a large appendix of reasoning/prompt artifacts, with strong explanatory intent but inconsistent scale, caption definitions, and global encoding semantics.
