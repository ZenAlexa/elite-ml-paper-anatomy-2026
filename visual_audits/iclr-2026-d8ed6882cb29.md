- **visual_narrative**：Figure 1 establishes the response-pair→SAE concept→expressed-preference pipeline. Table 1 and Figure 2 show dataset-specific effects and cross-dataset reversals; Figure 3 turns unsafe and subjective features into curation and personalization interventions. Appendix Table 2 tests the SAE against interpretability ablations, Tables 3–8 test embedding/seed, method, expert, and subjectivity robustness, Figures 4–7 provide AUC, explanation-match, Elo-failure, and τ consistency views, Tables 9–11 supply qualitative mechanism examples, Figures 8–9 expose the two LLM prompts, and Tables 12–18 provide complete feature inventories.
- **caption_system**：All objects use the label form “Figure/Table n:” followed by a prose caption. Main result captions (Figures 2–3 and Tables 1–2/5–6) combine setup with a stated finding and, when relevant, bootstrap or multiple-testing definitions. Figures 8–9 are prompt captions that identify the artifact but do not summarize its full protocol. Tables 13–18 point back to Table 12 for shared encoding definitions.

Table 5 adds a two-level dataset→S/J header; Tables 3–4 and 9–11 use paired or three-column qualitative headers; Table 7 pairs descriptions with βj/τj; Table 8 pairs demographic Group and Interpretation with scientific p-values. - **method_result_ablation_link**：Figure 1’s SAE interface leads to Table 2’s SAE-versus-Embed-TopDims/Embed-PCA ablation and Figure 4’s prediction AUC comparison. Figure 2 and Table 1 identify context conflicts; Figure 3 and Figure 6 test the anti-refusal intervention; Figure 3b, Table 7, and Figure 7 connect subjective-feature selection to estimator robustness. ## 8. 最终判断

- **most_reusable_patterns**：
  - A left-to-right measurable→interpretable→operational pipeline that makes the method interface legible before quantitative results.
  - Dataset-grouped natural-language feature tables with one effect column, one prevalence column, and a semantic status key.
  - A result chain that pairs aggregate comparison with concrete qualitative examples and publishes the exact LLM prompts used for interpretation/judging.
  - Before/after intervention plots use an explicit reference (zero, global model, or y=x) and keep the edit or baseline in the caption.
- **highest_value_objects**：
  - Figure 1: compactly defines WIMHF’s core data-to-action interface.
  - Figure 3: connects feature discovery to safety curation and sample-efficient personalization.
  - Figure 4 plus Table 2: puts prediction fidelity and the SAE interpretability ablation on compatible decision surfaces.
  - Figure 6 and Tables 10–11: make reward-hacking failure modes inspectable at model and example level.
  - Tables 12–18: preserve the complete interpretable feature inventories needed to reproduce cross-dataset claims.
- **failure_patterns**：
  - Several charts rely primarily on color and use small legends/labels, weakening grayscale and low-resolution reading.
  - Figures 2–3 and Figure 5 report bootstrap/error-bar results but do not expose all denominators or interval construction details in the object.
  - Figure 5’s aggregate caption says four most-active features while its feature-level panel visibly lists five rows; this count should be reconciled before reuse.
  - Qualitative tables and prompt screenshots are reproducible in content but omit run metadata such as seeds, model configuration, or judge temperature.
  - Dense full inventories provide point estimates and p-value coloring without feature-level uncertainty or sample-size columns.
- **one_sentence_visual_strategy**：The paper uses a human-readable SAE pipeline, dataset-specific colored feature inventories, intervention comparisons, and prompt-backed qualitative checks to turn opaque preference labels into inspectable curation, personalization, and reward-hacking evidence.
