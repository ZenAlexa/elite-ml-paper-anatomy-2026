## 最终判断

- **最可复用模式**：
  - Use a three-lane overview (clean pipeline, utility degradation, safety degradation) to align mechanism and headline outcomes before detail.
  - Use a fixed IF/ELSE prompt shell with direct in-box labels so a conditional attack interface is reproducible and auditable.
  - Use domain×evaluation or format×target matrices with direct cell values, baseline rows, and separators to show targeted diagonal effects and transfer.
  - Use small-multiple bar panels with a shared categorical x-axis for classifier/pathway robustness, then add appendix ablations for trigger, rate, and augmentation choices.
  - Use gray headed, monospaced prompt cards for exact templates and before/after text transformations, keeping method inputs separate from measured results.
- **最高价值对象**：
  - Figure 1: connects RR pipeline, two attacker objectives, and paper thesis.
  - Table 1 and Figures 4–6: quantify format/domain/entity/distribution utility degradation with clean baselines.
  - Figures 7–9: compare Omission Attack pathways, unseen-trigger transfer, classifiers, and HP semantic backdoors.
  - Figures 20–24 and Table 2: expose mechanism, ablation boundaries, cross-domain transfer, and verification quality.
- **失败模式**：
  - Heatmaps and multi-series bars depend heavily on color and small text; grayscale and narrow-column reading lose some series/domain distinctions.
  - Nearly all quantitative objects report point estimates without per-cell denominators, repeated-run variation, or uncertainty; Figure 20 is the exception with ±1 s.d. bands.
  - Prompt/template captions are often naming-only, so the visual must be read with surrounding prose to recover variables, model/version, sampling, and failure handling.
  - The paper’s public repository exists but contains no visual source, preventing source_exact reconstruction of palettes, plotting parameters, or table generators.
- **一句话视觉策略**：Build a clean-versus-poisoning narrative with pipeline and prompt interfaces, quantify targeted FPR/FNR flips through matrices and grouped bars, and use appendix templates, trigger transformations, mechanistic curves, and defenses to delimit reproducibility and transfer.
