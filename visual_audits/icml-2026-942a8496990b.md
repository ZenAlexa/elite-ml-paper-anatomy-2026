- **Visual narrative:** the main text moves from theorem decomposition (Figures 1–2), to representation alignment (Table 1), to subgrammar-location robustness (Figure 3), and finally to the recursive-depth failure boundary (Figure 4). ## Final judgment

- **Most reusable patterns:** theorem-facing paired small multiples with inspectable decompositions; matched location/depth/prefix panels with a common axis grammar; raw trajectories behind a heavier aggregate; multi-level result headers with explicit seed-pair denominators; compact booktabs configuration tables.
- **Highest-value objects:** Figure 1 for the paper’s conceptual mechanism; Table 1 for the central representation result; Figure 4 for the strongest failure boundary; Figure 6 for showing distributional rather than only average behavior.
- **Failure patterns:** captions generally omit the main finding and raw-run/whisker semantics; hue is overused without consistent shape/line redundancy; Figure 6 panels use different y ranges and panel a has lower raster resolution; Tables 1 and 4 leave percentage-change and “most significant” definitions unresolved; source repository scripts are only partial matches to the rendered PDF.
- **One-sentence visual strategy:** The paper uses theorem-linked, color-coded small multiples to move from additive subgrammar loss to representation and recursion-depth evidence, with appendix tables and raw-run/distribution views supplying factor sensitivity but leaving several statistical and rendering conventions implicit.
