## 跨对象系统与结论

- **Visual narrative**：The visual narrative starts with Figure 1’s perturbation insertion interface, moves to the headline dilution grid (Figure 2), and then grounds the benchmark boundary with the main MIA Table 1 and unlearning Figure 3. Appendix Figures 4–12 expand copyright, direct/indirect privacy, and test-set contamination; Figures 13–16 test timing, paraphrase transfer, and architecture; Tables 2–10 provide dataset, model, benchmark, and prompt contracts; Tables 11–15 and Figures 17–23 close MIA, unlearning, size, and interference boundaries.
- **Caption system**：Captions consistently begin “Figure/Table N:” and usually sequence title → experimental setup → encoding/metric key → comparison or finding. The strongest captions (Figures 2, 7, 13–16, 22–23; Tables 1, 9–10) define the intervention or point to companion objects. Several appendix captions are terse (Figure 10) or retain typographical errors (“paraphased”, “Defitions”), and uncertainty definitions are rarely in captions.
- **Table header system**：Tables use bold serif headers, grouped horizontal rules, and compact super-headers. Exposure bins (Dup ≠ 0, 1, 4, 16, 64, 256) recur as a stable column contract for MIA/count tables; scale groups recur in benchmark tables; prompt/configuration tables switch to descriptive columns and monospace blocks.
- **Method/result/ablation**：Figure 1 defines the data intervention; Figures 2, 4–12 quantify domain-specific memorization; Figures 13–16 vary timing, paraphrase, and architecture; Tables 3–5 and 15 expose counts/configurations; Figures 17–21 test MIA and unlearning operating behavior. The links are strongest when the same duplicate axis and model legend recur.
- **Main/appendix link**：The three main figures and Table 1 state dilution, MIA, and unlearning claims. Appendix D decomposes risk domains, Appendix E varies timing/paraphrase/architecture, Appendix F expands MIA, Appendix G expands unlearning, and Appendix I checks model size and domain interference. Every appendix object is traceable to a main claim or design contract.
- **Typography consistency**：Nimbus Roman No9 L and Computer Modern math dominate PDF text; DejaVu/monospace fonts appear in embedded plots and prompt tables. The outer caption and table hierarchy are stable, but dense 28-panel grids use small embedded labels and mixed font families.
- **Color consistency**：Purple/green condition roles recur across notebook-backed core plots, while MIA and unlearning introduce local categorical palettes. Marker/stroke redundancy is systematic for standard versus perturbed, but five-condition interference plots, timing windows, and prompt highlights are not reliably grayscale-safe.

### 最终判断

**最可复用模式**
- Use a fixed duplicate-bin x-axis and a shared standard/perturbed marker-stroke contract across domain small multiples.
- Pair a headline aggregate grid with domain/metric/attack-strength expansions so the central effect and its heterogeneity remain linked.
- Define intervention interfaces and attack prompts as standalone diagrams/tables before asking result figures to carry their semantics.
- Anchor trade-off claims with explicit reference points (desired/standard/perturbed) and expand them in a retain-set×evaluation grid.

**最高价值对象**
- Figure 1: concise, auditable interface for the perturbation operation.
- Figure 2: headline dilution result with cross-domain repetition and corpus-scale contrast.
- Figure 3: target-anchored unlearning benchmark with explicit reference models.
- Tables 1, 9, and 15: MIA metric/exposure contract, attack-prompt contract, and unlearning-search contract.

**失败模式**
- Dense appendix composites (Figures 8, 14, 16, 22, 23) make local ticks, legends, and error bars difficult to inspect.
- Most quantitative objects are point-estimate curves or tables without visible replicate counts or uncertainty definitions.
- Color carries model size, timing, domain condition, or prompt role in different local palettes; grayscale and color-vision robustness is partial.
- A few captions are terse or grammatically defective, and several findings use visual overlap/matching without an explicit tolerance.

**一句话视觉策略**：Hubble uses a stable duplicate-bin small-multiple grammar to move from a concrete perturbation interface through dilution and domain-specific leakage to timing, architecture, MIA, unlearning, and interference boundaries.
