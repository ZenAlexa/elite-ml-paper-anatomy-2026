## 全文版式与视觉系统

- 主文 Figure/Table 为 6/5，附录为 10/19。
- The paper uses a saturated categorical palette for method/variant identities, red emphasis for UniImb/best values, blue underlining or fill for runners-up, and pastel severity fills in construction tables. Colors are often reinforced by direct labels, hatches, marker shapes, or row placement, but the overall system is not grayscale-safe.
- PDF captions, body text, table rules, and many table glyphs are vector PDF objects; the architecture and plotted panels are embedded raster/composite assets or rasterized chart objects. The 200 dpi render and pdfimages inspection therefore support a mixed vector/raster assessment.
- Table headers, mean±std notation, red-best/blue-runner conventions, and dataset order are consistent across main and appendix tables. Figure families reuse red/blue sensitivity and colored ablation bars, but architecture diagrams, polar charts, 3-D surfaces, and qualitative embeddings use different typography and visual idioms; appendix density is substantially higher than the main text.

- **visual_narrative**：The visual narrative proceeds from the full UniImb mechanism (Figure 1) to prior selection and benchmark comparisons (Tables 1–3), then to efficiency, intertwined imbalance, ablation, sensitivity, transfer, and qualitative representation evidence (Figures 2–6 and Table 4–5). Appendix objects expand distribution/data construction, severity tiers, backbone coverage, hyperparameters, parameter interactions, and prototype interpretation.
- **caption_system**：Captions use a short object title followed by setup or comparison context. Long benchmark captions define mean±std, run count, and significance only for selected tables; most chart captions identify panels but leave uncertainty, scales, and direct-label semantics to the body or visual itself. Several captions retain typographic/grammar defects such as “in an uniform,” “of on,” and “wiht.”
- **table_header_system**：The dominant table grammar is grouped dataset headers with paired Macro-F1/Micro-F1 subcolumns, plus a left method/backbone column. Construction tables switch to nested count/size/ratio headers; hyperparameter tables use one flat parameter header. This change is semantically appropriate but requires careful caption/header reading.
- **method_result_ablation_link**：Figure 1’s topology encoding, perturbation, GNN, DBP, and load-balance lanes map to the named ablations in Figure 4 and Figures 8–10, while Figures 5 and 11–14 vary K/TopK settings introduced in the same diagram. Tables 22–24 then isolate backbone and DBP additions.
- **main_appendix_link**：Main Tables 1–5 and Figures 1–6 carry the claim spine; Appendix Tables 6–24 and Figures 7–16 provide prior replication, data/split definitions, severity tiers, hyperparameters, full backbone matrices, and qualitative mechanism checks. Exact author assets are available for only five objects, so PDF-rendered observations remain authoritative for the rest.
