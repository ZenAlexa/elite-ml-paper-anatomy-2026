- **visual_narrative**: The visual narrative moves from Figure 1’s five-scenario/model-scale headline to Figures 2–3’s architecture/API, Figures 4–6’s train-time mechanism and coverage, Table 1 plus Figures 7–8’s result/scaling/efficiency evidence, then Appendix Figures 9–22 and Tables 5–8 for curriculum intuition, reproducibility UI, task slices and qualitative successes/failures.

T1/T5–T8 use percentage values and two-decimal appendix precision (T1 native PDF is mostly one decimal); T2–T4 use compact task/ablation headers. Grouping is visually consistent but task-count/aggregation definitions remain in body text.

- **main_appendix_link**: Table 1 and Figure 1 provide the headline while Tables 5–8 provide WebArena/TextCraft/BabyAI/SciWorld slices; Figures 9 and 11 diagnose the method mechanism; Figures 12–22 expose selected successes and failure boundaries. Appendix H is especially useful for explaining why aggregate TextCraft gains do not imply universal long-horizon reliability.
- **typography_consistency**: Native tables consistently use Nimbus Roman/Monospace PDF objects and bold/italic group labels. Charts use small DejaVu/Arial-like labels, while conceptual/qualitative objects use Times/Consolas-like text. The regimes are internally coherent but chart legends, UI screenshots and trace text often fall below comfortable two-column reading size.
- **color_consistency**: Orange/red/green/blue/purple method and outcome accents recur across plots and traces, and gray group bands recur across tables. Color is often reinforced by labels, hatches, markers or column placement, but many chart comparisons still rely primarily on color and are not reliably grayscale-safe.

## Final judgment
- **most_reusable_patterns**:
  - Headline-to-decomposition pairing: a compact cross-scenario chart followed by task-level benchmark tables with Overall and consistent model groups.
  - Mechanism panel pairing: performance curve next to interaction/resource or gradient diagnostic, plus a separate progressive-curriculum schematic.
  - Selected qualitative cases use aligned Baseline versus ScalingInter-RL trace columns, explicit task goals and terminal scores, then trajectory montages for path/action/feedback.
  - Consistent bold/underline best-versus-second-best and gray row-group bands make wide benchmark tables scannable without vertical rules.
- **highest_value_objects**:
  - Figure 1
  - Figure 4
  - Table 1
  - Figure 8
  - Figure 9
  - Table 8
  - Figures 17–18
- **failure_patterns**:
  - Dense small legends, long model names and color-heavy charts reduce paper-scale and grayscale readability.
  - Most plots and all benchmark tables omit replicate counts and uncertainty definitions; Figure 6 shows a band without saying what it represents.
  - Selected qualitative traces lack sampling/selection metadata and cannot quantify prevalence; captions 5–8 and 19–22 are too terse for standalone interpretation.
  - Figure 14 caption describes a banana/purple-box task while the rendered task is sodium-chloride conductivity; Figure 15 has “1.0., while” punctuation.
  - Zero values in Table 8 are not explicitly labeled as failure versus missingness, although surrounding text explains the all-zero Chem-Mix limitation.
- **one_sentence_visual_strategy**: Use a compact multi-environment headline, modular architecture and progressive-horizon mechanism panels to motivate ScalingInter-RL, then connect benchmark tables, resource/scaling diagnostics and appendix trace cases so aggregate gains and their failure boundaries are visible in one consistent but deliberately mixed visual system.
