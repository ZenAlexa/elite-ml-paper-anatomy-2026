## Final judgment

- 最可复用模式：
  - Full-width what/when method schematic with explicit component letters and an outcome card.
  - Shared-axis family-coded line panels using hue, marker, and line style as redundant identity channels.
  - Within-model taxonomy heatmaps with common layout, explicit best/worst outlines, dead-state hatching, and a selection caption.
  - Headline tables that combine absolute triplets with a relative gap-recovery measure and define aggregation in the caption.
  - Negative-control bars with a zero reference, direct labels, and one hatch for the reference pipeline.
  - Appendix longtables with repeated semantic headers and fixed category/description/example columns.
- 最高价值对象：
  - Figure 1 — clearest method interface and visual anchor.
  - Table 1 — central absolute/relative reconstruction result across models and benchmarks.
  - Figure 2 — taxonomy-selection search and normalization caveat.
  - Figure 4 — component-level negative controls with directionally interpretable failures.
  - Figure 5 — readable qualitative bridge from sparse intervention to a solved example.
- 失败模式：
  - Within-panel normalization in Figure 2 limits cross-model score comparison.
  - Dense multi-column Table 1 and long Appendix G tables require small type and heavy scanning.
  - Figure 3 and several appendix tables omit uncertainty or sample-size context for visual differences.
  - Figure 5 is a single selected case with color-dependent, rasterized small text.
  - Public table generators and the appendix generator are stale relative to the final PDF layout, so source support is partial.
- 一句话视觉策略：以全宽 what/when 分解建立方法接口，再用 taxonomy heatmap、holdout loss、绝对/相对恢复表、稀疏度与负对照逐层锁定机制，最后以附录长表和单例 trace 提供可复核细节。
