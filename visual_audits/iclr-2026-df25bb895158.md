## Paper-level style

- Main objects: 9 figures / 3 tables; appendix objects: 5 figures / 4 tables.
- Palette: Main method diagrams use teal Context/Cache Attention and yellow Query Attention; locality maps use a green sequential scale; LPD is repeatedly accented in red in plots and cyan in result rows. Natural image montages use incidental image colors with red edit boxes. The table accent is method-row highlighting rather than a universal metric palette.
- Rendering: The 24-page PDF mixes vector text/rules/most diagrams and charts with raster attention maps and image montages. Figure 1 and Figure 12 match public PNG assets; Figures 2, 10, 11, 12, and 13 contain raster image content, while Figures 3–8, 9, and 14 are primarily vector/text compositions. All 24 pages were rendered as 1530×1980 PNGs at 180 dpi for inspection.
- Consistency: Nimbus Roman/Computer Modern dominate captions, body text, and tables; Helvetica Neue-like text appears inside diagrams and plots. Bold caption titles are consistent for main figures/tables and Figures 12–14, while title-only Figure 10/11 captions are weaker. Teal/yellow masks, green locality maps, red LPD marks, and cyan LPD table rows form a recognizable but locally scoped visual system.

## Final judgment

### most_reusable_patterns
- Start with a direct quality–latency scatter, then provide a wide table with matched model/step/cost rows so the headline can be audited.
- Use matched baseline/proposed attention-mask and cache matrices with role colors, labels, and one worked visibility example.
- Make a mechanism diagnostic and its operational schedule adjacent: Figure 7 measures locality while Figure 8 shows how it changes order selection.
- Organize ablations from architecture control to schedule substitute to joint principles, and follow with one-parameter threshold sensitivity.
- Plot paired cost curves across batch regimes and state the OOM stopping rule to delimit efficiency claims.
### highest_value_objects
- Figure 1 plus Table 1, because they connect the headline quality–latency frontier to complete system-level rows.
- Figures 3–6, because the token/query, mask, cache, and prior-method contrasts form the clearest method interface.

### failure_patterns
- No figure or table reports uncertainty, seeds, replicate counts, or statistical aggregation beyond the stated FID sample count and latency averaging protocol.
- Figures 10/11 are dense 24-head arrays with title-only captions; layer, sample, step, and aggregation identities remain implicit.
- Qualitative editing and text-to-image montages demonstrate capability without success/failure denominators or prompt/sample linkage.
