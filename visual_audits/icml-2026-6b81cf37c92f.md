## Final judgment

- **Most reusable patterns:**
  - Use Figure 2’s three-stage pipeline to align rollout context, decomposed objectives, and update direction with the corresponding equations.
  - Use Figure 3’s matched component-pair panels, VDS stress bins, orthogonality reference, and uncertainty bars to expose a geometric mechanism before the performance table.
  - Use Figure 4’s 2×2 loss layout to put the optimized visual objective beside the protected language-prior collateral objective.
  - Use Table 1/2’s grouped method headers, paired metrics, and adjacent deltas, while adding seeds, denominators, and named dispersion.
  - Use Figure 10’s dependency-stratified repeated intervention layout and Appendix F’s matched qualitative cards to connect heterogeneous quantitative and qualitative evidence.
- **Highest-value objects:**
  - Figure 2 (p.2) for the method/equation interface.
  - Figure 3 (p.4) for the VDS-conditioned gradient mechanism and LP motivation.
  - Table 1 (p.6) for the exact 2B/4B benchmark comparison.
  - Figure 4 (p.5) plus Figure 7 (p.13) for mechanism dynamics and the fixed-normalization assumption.
  - Figure 10 (p.16) and Appendix F card pairs (pp.19–24) for localization and qualitative grounding evidence.
- **Failure patterns:**
  - Most chart objects are raster composites with small embedded labels, color-only method encoding, and no public plotting source.
  - Point estimates and trajectories generally omit seeds, replicate dispersion, denominators, or timing protocols; the captions often state stronger directional findings than the visible uncertainty supports.
  - Figure 5 has an unresolved dashed-reference legend (“Teacher Model”) versus the central Standard OPD annotation, and Figure 8 presents λ values in a nonnumeric order.
  - Tables 1 and 2 place accuracy comparisons in a wide dense header but do not join uncertainty, cost, or sample-size metadata on the same decision surface.
  - Appendix F’s eight selected cards vividly show failure correction but have no selection protocol, image provenance, or population-level error estimate; the standard X-ray example is internally contradictory and the VGS inference is not independently validated.
- **One-sentence visual strategy:** The paper uses a teal/blue/magenta visual spine to move from decomposed VLM objectives through VDS-conditioned geometry and loss dynamics to benchmark, RL, sensitivity, cost, dependency, and qualitative evidence, with strong local explanation but limited reproducibility from small raster charts, color dependence, and missing uncertainty/source files.
