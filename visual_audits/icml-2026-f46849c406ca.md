## 跨对象系统判断

- **视觉叙事**：Figure 1 establishes baseline training collapse (success falls as KL and gradient norms rise); Figure 2 supplies the rollout/TTI/TDS interface; Figure 3 motivates the fused uncertainty geometry and Figure 4 turns it into a token cutoff example. Figure 5 is the main performance/efficiency headline linked to Tables 1–4, while Appendix Figures 6–7 and Tables 5–8 extend response-length, ALFWorld, sensitivity, staleness, hyperparameter, and reward-decomposition checks. The PDF’s §5 prose incorrectly calls the main stability/efficiency panels “Figure 7”, although the main composite is Figure 5 and Figure 7 is the appendix ALFWorld figure.
- **Caption 系统**：Captions use an italic Figure/Table label followed by sentence-style prose. Figure 1, Figure 3, Figure 4, and Figure 5 state a mechanism or finding; Figure 2 and Figure 7 are terse setup-only captions. Most captions omit sample denominators and uncertainty definitions; Figure 5 names bands/curves only indirectly and Figure 7 does not define its encodings.
- **表头系统**：Tables use compact booktabs or partial-grid rules, bold proposed/best cells, and grouped rows. Tables 1–2 use multi-level benchmark headers; Tables 3–4 and 6–8 use compact outcome columns; Table 5 uses italic lifecycle group rows. Units and uncertainty notation are inconsistently explicit, especially in Tables 1 and 8.
- **方法—结果—消融链**：Figures 2–4 map equations and control points to TTI/TDS; Figure 5 and Table 1 test the headline performance and efficiency claim; Tables 3–4 delete core modules or replace them with alternative controls; Table 8 decomposes reward outcomes to connect the mechanism to fine-grained task progress.
- **正文—附录链**：Main Figure 5’s token/turn efficiency is extended by Appendix Figure 6’s response-length/clipping settings and Figure 7’s ALFWorld replication. ## 最终判断

### 最可复用模式
- Failure triptych: align an outcome curve with internal diagnostics and a bounded highlighted failure interval.
- Method interface: connect rollout states to token-level and turn-level decisions using matched boxes, direct labels, and arrows.
- Efficiency composite: combine outcome, token-budget distribution/success ratio, and interaction turns with explicit budget reference lines.
- Mechanism-to-evidence chain: follow a metric contour comparison with a signal trajectory and one executable token-level example.

### 最高价值对象
- Figure 5, because it puts task performance and token/turn cost on one decision surface.
- Figure 2, because the four-box interface is the clearest bridge from equations to implementation and has the only verified public visual asset.
- Table 1, because it joins WebShop and ALFWorld headline outcomes across prompting, SFT, and two RL backbones.
- Figure 3 plus Figure 4, because they make the fused uncertainty signal and TTI trigger mechanistically inspectable.

### 失败模式
- Uncertainty, seed aggregation, run counts, and denominators are frequently omitted from captions and tables.
- Table 8 mixes decimal fractions with percentage-like reward values, weakening direct comparison.
- Table 2’s MuSiQue prose claim conflicts with the displayed values.
- The main-results prose contains a Figure 7 cross-reference for the Figure 5 stability/efficiency content.
- Rasterized small legends, token text, and color-only scalar encodings reduce accessibility and auditability.

**一句话视觉策略**：The paper uses a failure-to-mechanism-to-efficiency narrative: diagnose unstable exploration, diagram two uncertainty controls, visualize their signal/cutoff logic, then validate performance and resource efficiency with grouped benchmarks, ablations, and appendix stress tests.
