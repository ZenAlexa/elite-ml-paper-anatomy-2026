## Paper-level visual style

This is a single-column ICLR paper with page-width raster composites for every Figure and native PDF text/rules for every Table. Captions and tables use Nimbus Roman No9 L with Computer Modern math; raster composites introduce estimated sans-serif labels. Caption titles are consistently bold. Figure 1–3 use a blue/orange instructional palette with white/gray structure, while Figure 3 additionally uses green/magenta to distinguish within-/outside-clip sampling. Figure 5 uses orange/gray/blue stacked preference bars. Qualitative Figures 4 and 6–13 use the image content's photographic or illustrated colors, white gutters, and sparse black direct labels. Color is generally paired with panel/row labels, but most image montages are not grayscale-safe. Figures are raster XObjects (the smallest is Figure 3 at about 181 ppi in the PDF object); table text and rules are vector/PDF objects. The visual grammar is therefore strong for method explanation and qualitative narrative but weak for uncertainty, denominator, replication, and quantitative reproducibility.

## Cross-object system

- **Visual narrative:** Figure 1 frames the dual-system claim; Figure 2 makes the Analyzer/Planner–MMDiT interface concrete; Figure 3 motivates conditioning choices. Tables 1–4 establish component and benchmark comparisons, Figures 4–5 provide generalization and preference examples, and Appendix Figures 6–12 extend reasoning/long-form/semantic comparisons. Figure 13 is the only explicit failure plate. The narrative is coherent but leans heavily on qualitative exemplars for semantic claims.
- **Caption system:** All captions use a bold title phrase followed by regular Nimbus Roman prose. Main captions for Figures 1–6 and Tables 2–4 perform setup/comparison work; Appendix Figures 7–11 are title-only, reducing standalone interpretability. Figure 10/11 duplicate the reflection caption, and Figure 6 claims to provide reasoning process although the reasoning appears on later pages.
- **Table header system:** Direction arrows are consistent (`↑`/`↓`); Tables 3 and 4 group subjective/quantitative or dataset blocks, while Tables 1/2/5/6 use compact method/metric headers. Bold full-model/best cells communicate selection, but underline semantics and missing subjective values are undocumented.
- **Method–result–ablation link:** Table 1 explicitly isolates agentic reasoning and conditioning; Table 2 adds human semantic/artifact evidence; Tables 3–4 test multi-person and benchmark transfer. Appendix Figures 8–11 make PLF, MM-Warmup, and reflection visually inspectable, although the Figure 10 reference/caption mismatch breaks one link.
- **Main–appendix link:** Main Figures 1–5 and Tables 1–4 point to Appendix C/D/E; Appendix C gives evaluation details, D gives reasoning/MLLM prompt, E gives qualitative ablations, F gives latency, and H gives failure cases. The appendix expands visual coverage but does not add uncertainty or denominators.
- **Typography consistency:** Nimbus Roman No9 L and Computer Modern math are stable for native PDF captions/tables. Embedded raster labels vary in sans-serif sizing and can become small, especially in Figures 2, 5, 7, and 11. Figure 3 is visibly lower-resolution than the other composites.
- **Color consistency:** Blue/orange encode System 1/System 2 and method modules in Figures 1–3; later quantitative table semantics are black/white with arrows and bold/underline; qualitative figures inherit scene colors. Role labels generally provide redundancy, but there is no global color legend and grayscale accessibility is weak for Figures 1, 3, 5, and most image montages.

## Final judgment

- **Most reusable patterns:** (1) dual-panel method diagrams with an end-to-end pipeline beside enlarged component mechanisms; (2) fixed-column qualitative grids that align baseline/proposed outputs or long-form time points; (3) table headers that group subjective and objective evidence while preserving metric direction arrows; (4) candid limitation plates paired with the headline qualitative evidence.
- **Highest-value objects:** Figure 2 (method interface), Table 1 + Table 2 (component and semantic ablation), Table 3 (multi-person decision surface), Figure 8/9 (PLF mechanism), and Figure 13 (failure boundary).
- **Failure patterns:** qualitative selection without denominator/timestamp/prompt; no uncertainty or replicate information in any table/plot; title-only appendix captions; rasterized tiny labels; Figure 10's MM-Warmup/reference mismatch; duplicated reflection captions; and reliance on a few favorable examples for semantic superiority claims.
- **One-sentence visual strategy:** The paper uses a blue/orange dual-system story, compact objective/subjective tables, and dense qualitative montages to argue that MLLM planning plus multimodal diffusion improves context-aware avatar motion, but it should bind every qualitative claim to sampling/protocol metadata and uncertainty while repairing the Figure 10/11 caption linkage.
